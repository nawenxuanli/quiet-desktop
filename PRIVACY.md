# Quiet Desktop privacy policy

Effective October 29, 2026.

Quiet Desktop is a desktop companion for Windows and macOS. It does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, telemetry, or a server to collect your information.

## Information stored on your device

The app stores your notes, favorite application paths, appearance preference, a resized copy of your selected photo, and its display crop settings locally. These are used only to provide the app's features. The app does not encrypt this data itself; access depends on your operating system's account and storage protections.

The app reads installed application shortcuts or standard application folders to show apps you can launch. It opens an image only when you choose it. Your original image is not modified. It opens files, folders, or apps in response to your actions.

## Sharing and network access

Quiet Desktop does not upload your notes, photos, or app shortcuts, and does not sell or share them. Your operating system, cloud photo provider, and applications you open have their own privacy policies and may use network connections independently. GitHub and Microsoft Store handle downloads, updates, and feedback under their own policies.

## Your choices

You can edit notes, remove favorite shortcuts, remove the saved photo through the app, or clear the app's local data. Uninstalling a portable copy may leave the local data folder behind. Store installations may offer Windows app data reset controls. Avoid placing sensitive information into a public bug report.

## Contact

For privacy questions or removal guidance, open an issue at https://github.com/nawenxuanli/quiet-desktop/issues without including private information. A maintainer can arrange a private follow-up if needed.
